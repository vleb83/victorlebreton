About

Victor Le Breton

I am a real estate fund analyst who writes. By day I work in institutional real estate finance — fund modelling, acquisitions, and investor reporting — and I bring that same rigour to every article I write on finance, investing and property.

Most finance content is written by generalists who don't understand a DCF, a free cash flow bridge, or why an interest-rate move ripples through valuations. I do. That means SEO-optimised content that is also correct — sourced, accurate on the numbers, and credible to a reader who actually knows the subject.

Over 96 orders and 84 five-star reviews on Fiverr, I've written company deep dives, investment explainers, and market pieces for finance blogs, newsletters, fintech and proptech clients across the UK, Germany and beyond — many of them repeat, ongoing collaborations. Every sample below is real, published work.
